Prepared Meals

Fresh, chef-prepared meals delivered right to your door — no grocery runs, no meal planning, no dishes. Just real food, made by hand, ready when you are.

How It Works

Each week, browse a rotating menu of chef-prepared entrées. Build your meal by choosing one protein and two sides, mixed and matched however you like — 1 carb + 1 vegetable, 2 carbs, or 2 vegetables.

Delivery Days

Choose Monday, Wednesday, or both:

  • Monday Delivery — covers you Monday through Wednesday
  • Wednesday Delivery — covers you Wednesday through Saturday

Orders must be placed by Sunday at 12 PM for Monday delivery, or Tuesday at 12 PM for Wednesday delivery.

The Details

  • Minimum 2 meals per delivery, per address
  • $5 delivery fee per delivery day
  • Delivery available within our local delivery zone

Every meal is freshly prepared, portioned, and ready for the week — real, satisfying food without the effort of cooking it yourself.
